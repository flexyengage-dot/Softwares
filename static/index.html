Payments — deposit & withdraw TEST MODE

⚠️ TEST / simulation mode. Deposits and withdrawals only touch the simulated wallet — no real money moves. To move real money you must connect your own licensed payment processor (e.g. Paystack) with your verified account and API keys.
Deposit (fund the bot)
In mock mode this instantly credits the wallet. With Paystack you'll be redirected to their secure checkout.
Withdraw to your bank account
Withdrawals are manual and recorded, with a minimum amount and a daily limit. There is no instant auto-payout — that only happens through your verified processor account.
—

Crypto wallet — Trust Wallet / Telegram Wallet TEST MODE

⚠️ TEST / simulation mode. No real crypto moves. To go live you need your own Telegram Wallet Pay store key, or a TON deposit address + hot wallet.
Deposit (top up from your wallet)
In mock mode this instantly credits the wallet. With Wallet Pay you pay inside Telegram; with TON you send to the deposit address.
Withdraw to your wallet
You must authorize every withdrawal — there is no silent auto-pull. Real payouts require your own hot wallet keys.

Trading platforms & broker connections store credentials · verify connectivity

⚠️ Connectivity only. You can store your broker/exchange credentials here and verify the connection (read-only) — but auto-trading is not wired and will not place any order. Nigerian bookmakers (Bet9ja, SportyBet, Betway…) have no API, so they cannot be linked.

Decision desk — you decide every trade the bot calculates, you approve

🧭 The bot calculates each opportunity (probability, confidence, expected value) and shows it to you. Nothing is placed until you approve it. It will only auto-place a real order on approval when the expectation is positive and a broker is wired — and never with real money unless you set it to live with your own keys.
